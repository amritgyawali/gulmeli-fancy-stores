Frequently Asked Questions by New Sellers

Everything you need to know about starting your online store on Daraz Nepal.

Opening a shop and listing products on Daraz Nepal is completely free. There are no registration fees, monthly hosting charges, or hidden subscription costs. You are only charged a small commission percentage when a customer successfully buys and receives your product.
Individual sellers can register right away using their Nepali Citizenship Card (Nagarikta) or National Identity Card (NID) and a personal bank account. However, if you register as a Business / Corporate Entity or sell under DarazMall, submitting a company registration and VAT/PAN certificate is legally mandatory in accordance with Nepal Government regulations.
You only pack the ordered item and attach the auto-generated shipping label from your Seller Center portal. Then drop the package off at any of Daraz Express (DEX) Hubs located across major cities in Nepal, or request a scheduled pickup if you are a high-volume seller. Daraz logistics manages the last-mile doorstep delivery and Cash-on-Delivery collection.
Payments are automatically calculated on a weekly cycle and deposited directly via electronic fund transfer (NCHL / IPS) into your registered Nepali commercial bank account every Wednesday. You will receive an itemized weekly settlement statement in your dashboard.
