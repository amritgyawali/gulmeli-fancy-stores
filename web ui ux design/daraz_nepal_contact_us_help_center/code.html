Navigate to Account > My Orders on Daraz app or web. Click on Track Package to see real-time updates from DEX (Daraz Express) or partner couriers (Nepal Can Move / Sundar Courier) with rider contact details once dispatched.
All DarazMall purchases enjoy a 14-day hassle-free return policy for authentic products, defects, or change of mind (on eligible categories). Marketplace products allow 7-day returns. Drop off the item at any DEX Hub for 100% free return shipping.
During checkout, select Digital Payment and choose eSewa or Khalti. You will be redirected securely to authenticate via MPIN/OTP. Bank discounts and bank day voucher codes are automatically deducted before the final balance debit.
eSewa & Khalti refunds are credited back within 24 to 48 hours. Visa/Mastercard credit or debit card reversals reflect within 5 to 7 Nepali banking working days, depending on issuing commercial banks (Nabil, NIC Asia, Global IME, etc.).
On any product page or order confirmation, look for the seller card and click "Chat Now". This launches an instant in-app messenger with the merchant to inquire about stock, sizing, or specs before ordering.
