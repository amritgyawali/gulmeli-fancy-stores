# Mobile Game Market by Genre (Android / Google Play focus), 2025–2026

> **Method note.** The research date is 2026-10-24. Direct page fetches to sensortower.com, appmagic.rocks, deconstructoroffun.com, gamedevreports.substack.com and azurgames.com were **blocked by the session's network proxy (HTTP 403 / DNS failure)**. Every finding below therefore comes from **search-engine result summaries of those pages**, not from reading the full pages. Treat exact figures as "reported by secondary summaries of the named source" and check them against the primary report before using them in investment-grade decisions.
>
> **Data-basis caveats.**
> - Sensor Tower and AppMagic revenue is **store IAP only** (App Store + Google Play). It **excludes Android in China, third-party Android stores, D2C/web-shop revenue and ad revenue**.
> - Newzoo uses a broader methodology, so its totals are much larger. For example, Newzoo puts 2025 mobile at about $113B versus Sensor Tower's about $82B.
> - Figures are gross or net depending on the source. GameDev Reports/AppMagic figures are net of store commission and taxes.

## Q1: Top-grossing and most-downloaded genres/subgenres (global and Google Play), with revenue share, download share and YoY growth

### Takeaway
Total mobile game IAP revenue is flat to slightly down: about $82B in 2025 (+1%) and about $40B in H1 2026 (−2% YoY). Downloads are falling hard: about −7% in 2025 and −12% in H1 2026. **Strategy (4X) is the #1 revenue genre and puzzle is #2.** In H1 2026, puzzle grew about 20% while strategy fell about 5%. 4X looks to have peaked in Q4 2025. RPG and mid-core are declining. Download charts are dominated by block/arrow/tap puzzles and hybrid-casual "Ready"-style simulators.

### Inferences
- The market is now a **zero-sum, monetization-depth game**. Installs are shrinking about 7–13% a year, so revenue growth comes from better monetization of existing players (puzzle + meta, 4X) rather than from new users.
- **Puzzle is the 2026 momentum genre.** It is #2 in revenue, the fastest-growing major genre in H1 2026 (about +20%), and the top of the download charts (Block Blast, arrow puzzles). Strategy remains the biggest revenue pool, but it is a saturated, UA-heavy, top-heavy segment past its peak.
- Google Play game revenue held up slightly better than iOS in early 2026. The decline in game downloads is concentrated on Android, which fits a story of emerging-market download saturation and lower UA spending.

- Casino's YoY growth and sports/racing genre totals for 2026 were not found.

## Q2: Fastest-growing subgenres

### Takeaway
**Hybrid-casual** is the clearest structural growth story: IAP up about 20–37% in 2025 depending on the source, and up about 23% to about $2.4B in H1 2026 while the overall market shrank. Within it, **hybrid-casual puzzle** (block, arrow, tap-escape, screw/sort with meta) leads. Other risers are **merge + narrative/decoration** (Gossip Harbor at record highs), **cozy life-sim** (Heartopia), **football/sports** (World Cup 2026 boost), **4X + idle/survival** hybrids (Kingshot), and **roguelite/"Capybara-like" hybrid RPGs** (Habby). 4X overall has peaked, and RPG and mid-core are declining.

### Cited Findings
- Hybrid-casual was **the only segment with meaningful IAP growth in 2025, +20% YoY to about $4.2B**, with hybrid-casual puzzle the standout. — [GameDev Reports SoG 2026](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-state-of-gaming-2026); [WN Hub](https://wnhub.io/news/analytics/item-50202). Another summary gives Sensor Tower's own hybrid-casual 2025 growth as +37% versus Game World Observer's +20%. — [Cinevva](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/casual-games-trends-2026). The figures conflict, probably because of taxonomy differences.
- Hyper-casual IAP was nearly $700M worldwide in 2025, up 130% YoY. This reflects hyper-casual titles adding IAP. — [Naavik podcast w/ Sensor Tower](https://naavik.co/podcast/the-state-of-gaming-in-2026-with-sensor-tower/)
- H1 2026: hybrid-casual IAP was about **$2.4B, +23% YoY**, while total mobile IAP fell 2%. — [Playio blog citing Sensor Tower](https://blog.playio.co/mobile-game-genres-list)
- Hybrid-casual monetization mix differs by cluster. **Lifestyle and puzzle hybrid-casual run 59% IAP / 41% IAA**; **action and strategy hybrid-casual run 81.9% IAP / 18.1% IAA**. — [GameDev Reports / Sensor Tower](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-state-of-gaming-2026)
- **Arrow/tap puzzles** were the breakout download mechanic of 2026, with three titles in the top-5 downloads by June 2026. — [GameDev Reports: AppMagic June 2026](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/appmagic-top-mobile-games-by-revenue-6bb)
- **Merge + story**: Gossip Harbor (Microfun) hit an all-time monthly revenue record in August 2026 and was #2 grossing worldwide in September 2026. — [GameDev Reports: AppMagic Aug 2026](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/appmagic-top-mobile-games-by-revenue-980); [AppMagic Sept 2026](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/appmagic-top-mobile-games-by-revenue-2a7)
- **Cozy life-sim**: Heartopia had more than 12M downloads in the two weeks after its January 2026 global launch. — [GameDev Reports: AppMagic Jan 2026](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/appmagic-top-mobile-games-by-revenue-11d); [MobileGamer.biz Q1 data digest](https://mobilegamer.biz/data-digest-q1s-top-performers-milestones-for-pokemon-tcg-pocket-heartopia-efootball-and-plenty-more/)
- **Farm/sim comeback**: Hay Day had a revenue comeback, flagged in MobileGamer.biz's H1 2026 digest. — [MobileGamer.biz](https://mobilegamer.biz/data-digest-sensor-towers-h1-analysis-hay-days-comeback-julys-top-games-big-krafton-numbers-and-more/)
- **Football/sports**: football game downloads surged around the 2026 World Cup. Soccer Superstar jumped 20 spots to #12 in downloads. eFootball reported an all-time revenue record in July 2026, tied to the World Cup. — [GameDev Reports: AppMagic July 2026](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/appmagic-top-mobile-games-by-revenue-ef2)
- **4X + idle/survival**: Kingshot (Century Games, launched February 2025) blends idle mechanics with 4X and survival. It earned more than $500M net in its first 11 months on App Store + Google Play and crossed $100M in 117 days. — [Appfigures](https://appfigures.com/resources/insights/kingshot-500m-revenue-century-games); [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/century-games-kingshot-crosses-100-million-in-just-117-days)
- **Roguelite / hybrid-casual RPG**: Habby has built a repeatable "hybridcasual template" (Archero → Capybara Go → Archero 2). — [Deconstructor of Fun, July 2025](https://www.deconstructoroffun.com/blog/2025/7/31/habbys-hybridcasual-empire-the-template-that-built-a-powerhouse)
- **Ad-based models**: rising ad prices have made hyper-casual "economically viable again" (2026). — [Deconstructor of Fun: State of Mobile 2026](https://www.deconstructoroffun.com/blog/2026/2/2/state-of-mobile-2026)

### Inferences
- The highest-growth zone combines a **simple core mechanic** (block, arrow, merge, tap) with a **progression or narrative meta** and a **mixed IAA+IAP** model. This is low-production-cost territory and suits small or indie teams better than 4X or RPG, which need $100M+ UA budgets.
- Tower defense, idle/incremental, social casino and party/battle royale did not surface as standout growth subgenres in 2026 data I could find. Battle royale is a mature, incumbent-locked category (PUBG Mobile, Free Fire).

### Gaps
- No 2026 numbers were found specifically for idle/incremental, tower defense, social casino growth, match-3 (beyond Royal Match's rank) or survivor-likes as a measured subgenre.
- I couldn't verify GameRefinery/Liftoff subgenre-level growth tables.

## Q3: Breakout hits of 2024–2026 and why they succeeded

### Takeaway
The 2025–2026 breakouts fall into four patterns:
- **4X-survival from Chinese studios** (Last War, Whiteout Survival, Kingshot) won with aggressive UA, misleading-but-effective "mini-game" ads, and deep whale monetization.
- **Puzzle/hybrid-casual** (Block Blast, Royal Match, arrow puzzles) won on mass reach and ads plus a meta layer.
- **IP-driven collectors** (Pokémon TCG Pocket) won on collection psychology.
- **Hybrid roguelites** (Archero 2, Capybara Go) won with a repeatable Habby template.

- The "why" differs by tier:

  - **Habby's titles** show that a roguelite/RPG skill-pick loop wrapped in casual art can be iterated into a portfolio.
- These causal explanations are partly analyst opinion rather than measured fact.

## Q4: Hyper-casual vs hybrid-casual in 2025–2026

### Takeaway
Pure hyper-casual's old "launch-to-offset-decline" model is considered dead by major publishers such as Voodoo. Hyper-casual still drives a large share of downloads, and ad-price rises have made it viable again. The money has moved to **hybrid-casual**: about $4.2B IAP in 2025 (+20%) and about $2.4B in H1 2026 (+23%). Classification is fuzzy, because many "hyper-casual" chart-toppers are really hybrid-casual puzzle games.

### Cited Findings
- Voodoo's CEO said the old model of launching new titles to offset decline "now belongs to the past," citing declines of up to 40% a year. — [Azur Games 2026 report](https://azurgames.com/blog/hypercasual-and-hybrid-casual-in-2026-full-report/)
- Analytics services sometimes classify hybrid-casual puzzle games as hyper-casual, which muddies segment data. Installs declined YoY, but revenue increased across most hyper/hybrid genres. — [Azur Games 2026 report](https://azurgames.com/blog/hypercasual-and-hybrid-casual-in-2026-full-report/); [Azur Games: what happened to hypercasual](https://azurgames.com/blog/what-happened-to-hypercasual-market-growth-over-the-past-year-and-where-it-stands-now/); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/what-happened-to-hypercasual-the-markets-evolution-over-the-past-year/)
- Azur Games frames hyper-casual in 2026 as "the rise of puzzle games, the decline of other genres, and competition with short-form video." — [Azur Games](https://azurgames.com/blog/hypercasual-and-hybrid-casual-in-2026-full-report/)
- Rising ad prices have made hyper-casual "if not great again, at least economically viable again." — [Deconstructor of Fun State of Mobile 2026](https://www.deconstructoroffun.com/blog/2026/2/2/state-of-mobile-2026)
- Hyper-casual IAP was about $700M in 2025 (+130% YoY), so hyper-casual titles are adding IAP layers. — [Naavik podcast](https://naavik.co/podcast/the-state-of-gaming-in-2026-with-sensor-tower/)
- Hybrid-casual IAP was +20% to about $4.2B in 2025 and +23% to about $2.4B in H1 2026. — [GameDev Reports SoG 2026](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-state-of-gaming-2026); [Playio](https://blog.playio.co/mobile-game-genres-list)
- The H1 2026 AppMagic casual report is titled "Growth Beneath the Still Surface," describing a flat-looking casual market with internal growth. — [AppMagic Casual Report H1 2026](https://appmagic.rocks/research/casual-report-H12026)
- January 2026: "casual market flat." — [MobileGamer.biz data digest](https://mobilegamer.biz/data-digest-kingshot-outpaces-whiteout-survival-female-players-overlooked-casual-market-flat-januarys-top-titles-more/)

### Inferences
- For a small team building on Android in 2026, a pure-IAA hyper-casual game is high risk because of thin margins and fast decay. The proven path is a **hybrid-casual** design: hyper-casual core, a meta layer, a battle pass or IAP, and rewarded ads. Puzzle and lifestyle hybrid-casual are more ad-friendly (about 41% IAA) than action and strategy hybrid-casual (about 18% IAA).

### Gaps
- No exact 2026 hyper-casual download share or IAA revenue figure was found. Ad revenue is poorly tracked by Sensor Tower and AppMagic.

## Q5: Regional differences in Android-heavy markets (Asia, LATAM, South Asia, Nepal/India)

### Takeaway
India is the world's largest download market: 8.45B installs in FY2024-25, about 15.7% of global downloads in December 2025. It monetizes weakly, with about $400M+ IAP. Downloads skew to **simulation (driving/bus/bike), arcade, puzzle and tabletop/board (Ludo)**. Revenue skews heavily to **shooters/battle royale (about 50% of IAP)**, then casino and strategy. Nepal, Pakistan and Bangladesh share the same pattern: **PUBG Mobile, Free Fire (MAX) and Mobile Legends**, plus **Ludo-style social board games**, on low/mid-range Android devices.

## Q6: Notable 2026 trends (AI content, UGC, web shops after Epic v. Google, PC/mobile cross-play)

### Takeaway
Four trends matter in 2026:
- **Web shops / D2C on Android are being unlocked.** The Epic v. Google injunction and a 2026 settlement allow alternative billing and link-outs in the US, with Google fees reportedly cut to about 10–20%. D2C is about 15% of mobile IAP, roughly $17B.
- **AI is pervasive in UA creatives.** More than half of top-100 grossing games use AI-assisted ads.
- **UGC is still Roblox-dominated**, and Roblox's mobile IAP is softening.
- **Cross-platform free-to-play** (Genshin-style, Honor of Kings) and cross-progression are retention tools for big studios.

### Inferences
- For a new Android developer, the Google fee cut and web-shop link-outs mostly benefit **IAP-heavy games with returning payers** (4X, merge, puzzle with coins). Their effect on ad-monetized casual titles is small. Outside the US, including Nepal and India, the standard Play Billing rules (15% on the first $1M under the existing programme) still largely apply, though this was not verified in this research.
- AI lowers the cost of making creatives and assets. That compresses the advantage of big UA teams somewhat, but it also floods the market with clones, which matters for easy-to-copy mechanics like arrow puzzles.
