Privacy Policy

Effective 9 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Gulmeli Fancy Stores Android app and website (together, the “Service”) collect, why, who it is shared with and how you can delete it. Gulmeli Fancy Stores (“we”, “us”) is an online shop delivering in Nepal. Contact: amritgyawali999@gmail.com.

We do not collect your precise or approximate location, contacts, call logs, SMS, or advertising ID, and we do not show third-party ads.

Device permissions

  • Camera — only when you choose to take a photo (profile picture, review photo or photo search). Photos stay on your device unless you upload them as a profile or review picture.
  • Photos — the Android system photo picker lets you pick a single image; the app cannot browse your gallery.
  • Notifications — order updates and optional offers.
  • Fingerprint / face unlock — optional app lock. Biometric data never leaves your device and is handled entirely by Android.

Payments

Orders are paid by cash on delivery. The app does not collect card or bank details.

Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that run the Service for us, under their own security and privacy terms:

  • Supabase — account sign-in and the database that stores your profile, orders and messages.
  • Cloudinary — storage and delivery of product images and uploaded profile photos.
  • Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivering push notifications and app updates.
  • Delivery partners — your name, phone and address for the orders you place.

We may disclose information if required by law in Nepal.

Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your session token is kept in the device’s secure storage. Database access rules ensure each customer can only read their own account, orders and messages.

How long we keep data

We keep your account data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, saved addresses, cart, wishlist, reviews, support messages and notification tokens are erased immediately. Records of completed orders are kept without your name or contact details because we need them for sales and tax records.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Account → Settings → Delete account. You can also request deletion without the app: see Delete your account.

Your choices and rights

  • View and correct your profile in Account → Edit profile.
  • Turn promotional notifications off in Settings or in Android settings.
  • Ask for a copy of your data or its deletion by emailing us.

Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
