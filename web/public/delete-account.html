Delete your Gulmeli Fancy Stores account

You can delete your account and its data at any time, from the app or by email.

Option 1 — in the app (immediate)

  1. Open the Gulmeli Fancy Stores app and sign in.
  2. Go to Account → Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account, then Delete my account to confirm.

If an order is still on its way, cancel it or wait until it is delivered first.

Option 2 — by email (no app needed)

Send an email from the address you signed up with to amritgyawali999@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Gulmeli account”. We confirm and complete the deletion within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in (email and password)
  • Name, phone number, delivery address and profile photo
  • Cart, wishlist, followed stores, browsing history and reviews
  • Customer care messages
  • Push notification tokens for your devices

What is kept

Records of completed or cancelled orders (items, prices, dates) are kept for our sales and tax records, with your name, phone and address removed so they no longer identify you. Uploaded profile photos are removed from our image storage within 30 days.
